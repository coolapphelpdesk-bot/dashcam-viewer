Privacy Policy

Dash Cam Viewer · last updated July 5, 2026

1. Summary

Dash Cam Viewer (“the app”) is a companion app that connects to a compatible Wi-Fi dashcam (Novatek-based car DVR) to show its live view, browse recordings, and download clips to your phone. It is published by Cool App Helpdesk.

  • The developer collects no personal data of any kind. There are no user accounts, no analytics, and no developer-operated servers.
  • All communication happens directly between your phone and your camera over the camera’s local Wi-Fi network. Nothing is sent to the developer or any third party.
  • The app displays no advertisements and contains no third-party tracking SDKs.

2. Data we collect

None. Dash Cam Viewer does not collect personal data, telemetry, identifiers, device information, IP addresses, advertising IDs, or usage analytics. There are no user accounts and no developer-operated backend. The app has no feedback form or crash-reporting service that transmits data to the developer.

5. Network activity and third parties

Dash Cam Viewer only makes network connections to your dashcam on the local Wi-Fi network (default address 192.168.1.254):

  • Camera control — HTTP requests to the camera’s local API (/?custom=1&cmd=…) to read status and start/stop recording.
  • Live view — an MJPEG or RTSP video stream served by the camera on the local network.
  • Recordings — the recording list, thumbnails, and file downloads are fetched directly from the camera.

These connections use the camera’s own HTTP protocol on your local network and never leave it. The app contacts no internet servers of its own and contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, social, or attribution SDKs. Bundled libraries (Media3 ExoPlayer, OkHttp, Coil, Room, DataStore) operate strictly within the scope described here.

6. Children’s privacy

Dash Cam Viewer is a general-purpose utility for dashcam owners. It is not directed at children under 13, but we recognize that minors may use it. The developer collects no personal information from any user, regardless of age.

The app contains no advertisements, no behavioral tracking, and no in-app purchases.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because the developer collects nothing, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on our end.

To remove locally stored data (saved cameras, preferences, cached thumbnails), clear the app’s storage in Android Settings → Apps → Dash Cam Viewer → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Clips you downloaded can be deleted from within the app’s album or through your file manager.

8. Security

The app stores settings and cached data in its private, sandboxed storage. Connection settings are kept in an encrypted DataStore. Communication with the dashcam uses the camera’s local HTTP protocol over the private Wi-Fi link between your phone and the camera; it does not traverse the internet. The app is distributed through the Google Play Store, which verifies the developer signing key on every update.

If any material aspect of how the app handles data ever changes, this page will be updated and the “last updated” date above will be bumped. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.
